Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Nederlands ↓

Who we are

Deals voor LEGO (Instagram, TikTok and Facebook @dealsvoorlego) is run by Ferdinand Brand, Alphen aan den Rijn, the Netherlands. Contact: kortinglego@outlook.com.

What our apps do

Our internal apps (“Deals voor LEGO poster” for Meta and “Deals voor LEGO” for TikTok) publish LEGO deal posts and videos to our own Facebook Page, Instagram business account and TikTok account @dealsvoorlego, using the official Meta Graph API and the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API. They are used only by the owner.

What data we process

We do not collect or store personal data of visitors or of other Facebook, Instagram or TikTok users. The apps do not read comments, messages, profiles or other user data. The only data processed are the access tokens and basic account info (e.g. account name) of our own accounts, stored securely and used only to publish to those accounts. This website uses no cookies or analytics.

Affiliate links and third parties

Our posts may contain Amazon affiliate links (Amazon.nl, Amazon.com.be). Amazon may set cookies and process data under its own privacy notice; we have no access to that data. We also use Linktree (linktr.ee/dealsvoorlego), covered by Linktree's privacy policy. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

Selling or sharing data

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties.

Data deletion and your rights

Because we store no user data, there is nothing to delete. You can still email kortinglego@outlook.com with a request for access or deletion (GDPR); we respond within 30 days. You can revoke app access on Facebook (Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites) or TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services).
