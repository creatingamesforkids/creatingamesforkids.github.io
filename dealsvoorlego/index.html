Daily LEGO deals for the Netherlands & Belgium

Deals voor LEGO is a small, independent deal channel run by Ferdinand Brand, Nederland. Every day we look for the best LEGO® discounts available in the Netherlands and Belgium (typically 35% off or more, or an all-time low price) and share them as short videos and posts on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.

Instagram @dealsvoorlego TikTok @dealsvoorlego Facebook All current deals (Linktree)

What we do

  • We check prices of LEGO sets at major retailers (mainly Amazon.nl and Amazon.com.be, plus official LEGO promotions).
  • Each selected deal gets a short video with the set, the old and new price and the discount.
  • Links to the deals are collected on our Linktree and short links on this site.

How we post to TikTok (Content Posting API)

Our app “Deals voor LEGO” is an internal tool used only by the owner to publish to our own TikTok account, @dealsvoorlego:

  1. The owner logs in once with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app to post to the @dealsvoorlego account.
  2. Our tool creates a deal video (product images, price and discount) and a caption.
  3. The video is uploaded to our own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API, with the caption and privacy setting chosen by the owner.

The app does not access, collect or store data of other TikTok users, does not read comments or messages, and is not offered to the public. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
